# 42 U.S.C. § 3604(c)

Part of the Fair Housing Act (Title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968), codified at 42 U.S.C. § 3601 et seq. Section 3604 lists the specific acts that are unlawful housing practices. Subsection (c) is the advertising provision. This is binding federal law, quoted verbatim.

## Verbatim text

<!-- verbatim:3604c -->
> To make, print, or publish, or cause to be made, printed, or published any notice, statement, or advertisement, with respect to the sale or rental of a dwelling that indicates any preference, limitation, or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status, or national origin, or an intention to make any such preference, limitation, or discrimination.
<!-- /verbatim -->

## What this means for an auditor

It is unlawful to publish an ad, notice, or statement about the sale or rental of a dwelling that:
1. **Indicates** a preference, limitation, or discrimination based on a protected class, OR
2. **Indicates an intention** to make such a preference, limitation, or discrimination.

"Indicates" is the operative word: the ad does not need to state a policy outright. Coded language, symbols, or phrasing that a reasonable reader would understand as signaling a preference is enough. The seven protected classes named in the statute are listed in [[protected-classes]].
